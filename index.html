Welfare lock-in

Near-universal entitlements have become a political floor. The report records ₹1,21,598 crore spent across the guarantees up to February 2026.

Capital constraint

A persistent revenue deficit narrows room for water, transit and higher-education investment—even while fiscal deficit and liabilities remain inside statutory bands.

Bengaluru

The fiscal asset—and governance liability

The country's deepest Global Capability Centre cluster and a software export engine sit inside a city whose civic institutions have not held a full municipal council election since 2015.

Economic depth

  • Software exports approach ₹5.5 lakh crore statewide.
  • GCCs span engineering, finance and AI research.
  • HAL-to-Infosys corporate depth reduces single-employer risk.
  • Stamp duty, GST, excise and salaries generate disproportionate own-tax revenue.

Governance impasse

  • Five corporations and 369 delimited wards under the 2024–25 Act.
  • No confirmed poll date or election roster as of the briefing.
  • A PIL invokes the 74th Amendment against an unelected super-municipality.
  • Responsibility for civic failure floats upward without an elected mayor.
